Vendored copy of the Xj3D parse-test corpus.

What this is
  263 .wrl files, 7019695 bytes, copied verbatim - no reflow, no re-encoding, no renames.
  They are the regression corpus: byte-exact round-trip, the diagnostic baseline and golden
  files, the formatter's idempotence pass and the large-scene performance budgets all read
  nothing else.

Where it came from
  <workspace>/xj3d/parsetest  (Xj3D's own parse tests), taken 2026-09-06.
  Only .wrl: the tests and the spec generator walk that extension and nothing else, so the
  upstream directory's 1029 files (360 .x3dv, 205 .java, 77 .x3d, textures, audio) came over
  as its 263 scenes, and the subdirectories that hold only those other files were not created.
  Digest of the copy, as `find . -name '*.wrl' | sort | xargs sha256sum | sha256sum`:
  42e7e39d26d13a7c...  Verify against a live checkout with:

    diff <(cd ../../xj3d/parsetest        && find . -name '*.wrl' -exec sha256sum {} + | sort) \
         <(cd vrml-lsp/parsetest && find . -name '*.wrl' -exec sha256sum {} + | sort)

Why it is here
  `mvn test` must work from this directory alone. The sibling checkout is not part of the
  project and is not guaranteed to exist on another machine.

Refreshing
  Re-run the copy the same way, then verify before committing:

    mkdir -p parsetest && (cd ../../xj3d/parsetest && find . -name '*.wrl' -print0 \
        | tar --null -cf - --no-recursion -T -) | tar -xf - -C parsetest

  Expect the corpus tests to change counts, not to fail silently: the round-trip and reflow
  tests print how many files they saw. Any new or edited scene also moves the diagnostic
  baseline, which is regenerated deliberately (see src/test/resources/corpus/).

Do not
  Edit these files to make a test pass. They are somebody else's scenes and the point of them
  is that nobody here has a stake in the outcome. Reproduce against the real Xj3D corpus with
  -Dparsetest.dir=../xj3d/parsetest if in doubt.
